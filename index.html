I. Reality Alignment

"Truth before preference."

Reality is the foundation of wisdom. Align perception with what is true.

  • Seek truth over comfort.
  • Update your mental models.
  • Question assumptions.
  • Observe before reacting.
Reflect: What am I refusing to see?

II. Sovereign Awareness

"I am the awareness behind experience."
  • Create space between stimulus and response.
  • Master attention and emotion.
  • Choose consciously.
Reflect: Am I acting from awareness or conditioning?

III. Creation Principle

"The creator shapes reality."
  • Create software • knowledge • art
  • Create systems • value
Reflect: What did I create today?
