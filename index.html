I

Reality Alignment

"Truth before preference."

Reality is the foundation of wisdom. Align perception with what is true rather than what is comfortable.

  • Seek truth over comfort.
  • Update your mental models continuousy.
  • Question core assumptions.
  • Observe before reacting.
Reflect: What am I refusing to see?
II

Sovereign Awareness

"I am the awareness behind experience."
  • Create space between stimulus and response.
  • Master attention and emotional flow.
  • Choose consciously from stillness.
Reflect: Am I acting from awareness or conditioning?
III

Creation Principle

"The creator shapes reality."
  • Create software, knowledge, & art.
  • Build systems & compound value.
Reflect: What did I create today?
